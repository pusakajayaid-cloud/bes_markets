About Pusakajaya Markets

The export marketing arm of Pusakajaya

Pusakajaya Markets is a division of PT Baksya Elok Saentuke (Pusakajaya), a legally incorporated Indonesian trading company. We focus on three high-value commodity lines: Vanilla, Cocoa & Chocolate, and Indonesian Spices.

We work as the bridge between Indonesian producers, farmer groups and cooperatives and international buyers. We take care of market access, quotations, export documentation and shipment coordination, so producers can focus on quality and buyers can source with confidence.

Our Product Lines

Vanilla, Cocoa & Spices from Origin

Sourced from trusted Indonesian producers, graded to buyer specifications and prepared for international markets.

Vanilla

Indonesian Vanilla

Rich, sweet and aromatic vanilla pods, carefully cured to preserve natural vanillin, ideal for gourmet, bakery, beverage and extraction use.

  • Gourmet grade (Grade A) whole pods
  • Extraction grade (Grade B) pods
  • Cuts & powder on request
  • Custom length, moisture & packing
🍫Cocoa & Chocolate

Cocoa & Chocolate

Indonesian cocoa from leading growing regions, from fermented beans to processed cocoa products for chocolate makers and food manufacturers.

  • Fermented & sun-dried cocoa beans
  • Cocoa nibs
  • Cocoa powder & cocoa butter
  • Chocolate products on request
🌿Spices

Indonesian Spices

The legendary Spice Islands heritage: authentic aroma and flavor, carefully handled from harvest to shipment.

  • Cloves
  • Nutmeg & mace
  • Cinnamon (cassia)
  • Pepper, cardamom & more on request
